App Suite Overview

One connected system for Daily Care Support Services: a ConnectTeam-style workforce app for Direct Support Professionals in the field, a secure eBinder & compliance records hub for the back office, and a portal for the people and families being supported. Every screen below is live and clickable — use the Prev/Next bar (or arrow keys, or swipe on mobile) to step through a flow in order.
